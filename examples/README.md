# Synthetic example

`curved-demo.csv` matches the current built-in curve-fitting demonstration. It is not a laboratory measurement or a thermodynamic fluid simulation.

The generating curves meet at volume 35 cc and pressure 1000 psi gauge. A useful manual test assigns volumes below 34.7 to the high branch, above 35.3 to the low branch, and leaves the three intervening points out of both fits. The ideal noiseless quadratic intersection is exactly 35 cc / 1000 psi.

The optional phase selection supplies context only; these numbers do not validate bubble-point, dew-point or lower-boundary physics.
